One semester left: finals, the capstone defense, and what's next

2026-05-08 · 4 min read · life · gmu · recruiting

Spring 2026 finals start Monday. Senior Design is officially shipped — we had the final poster session in April, and our team came away with a 2nd Place plaque in the AI-Enabled Vulnerability Research track. There's a longer retro on that coming, but this post is about the smaller question: what does the last semester actually look like?

What's on the calendar

  • Cryptography Fundamentals final — written exam, plus a take-home where I have to break a deliberately weak AES-CBC implementation. I've been writing breakers in CryptoLab as study tooling, which has been weirdly fun.
  • Cyber Vulnerability final project — threat model + write-up of a containerized web stack. Combining the threat-modeling notes from Avint with what I learned writing HackFax CTF challenges.
  • Systems Security & Resilience — cumulative final. I'm spending most of my time here on the parts I'm shakiest on, which is honestly always paging and scheduling theory.
  • Renovics tickets in the background. People still need their laptops to work in May.

Fall 2026: the last one

Fall is when I close out the degree. The two courses I'm most looking forward to are Critical Infrastructure Protection and a senior elective on Secure RF Communications. I want to spend the semester deepening the stuff I only got introductions to during capstone: more rigorous threat modeling, a real grasp of detection engineering, and an actual NIST RMF walk-through end to end.

I also want one more public artifact before graduation. Probably a write-up of how the AVINT pipeline performs against a held-out set of vulns from 2024 that were catalogued late — can our system surface them earlier than NVD did? That's the eval I keep wanting to run.

Recruiting

I'm interviewing for Summer 2026 internships and looking ahead to new grad roles that start after December. SOC analyst, AppSec, GRC, AI security, and IT engineering are all on the table — I'm not narrowing yet. If you're hiring or know a team that is, say hi — my resume's in the nav.
