AVINT — RAG Risk Enumeration of Uncatalogued Vulnerabilities

2nd Place · CYSE Senior Design Competition 2026

Year-long senior design with Avint LLC. We built an AI pipeline that mines unstructured threat intel from GitHub, Exploit-DB, and Seclists RSS, runs it through an NLP/NER chunker, and pushes the embeddings into a Llama 3 vector store. On top of that sits a modified DREAD risk scorer that produces 0–100 assessments for vulnerabilities that haven't been catalogued in the CVE database yet. The point: shorten the window between discovery and triage.

AccessAudit

A Python utility for parsing physical access control logs and surfacing the anomalies a human reviewer would otherwise miss: out-of-hours badge-ins, credentials used at two readers within an impossible travel window, and gaps where surveillance coverage should have caught a transit but didn't. Inspired by the manual audit work I do at the QTS Tier III+ campus. Inputs are vendor-agnostic CSV; output is a structured anomaly report.
